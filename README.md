# Electronics-Engineering-Library

A curated collection of essential books, references, and specifications on Electronic Engineering, Circuit Design, Signal Integrity, and Embedded Systems.

---

## 📚 Book List & Folder Structure

### 📁 [01_Circuit_Design](./01_Circuit_Design/)
Focuses on fundamental and advanced analog/digital circuit design, component behavior, and practical hardware engineering:
* **The Art of Electronics (3rd Edition)** — *Paul Horowitz, Winfield Hill*
* **Analog Circuit Design (Volumes 1, 2, & 3)** — *Jim Williams, Bob Dobkin*
* **Digital Electronics** — *Thomas L. Floyd*
* **Electronic Devices and Circuit Theory (11th Edition)** — *Robert L. Boylestad, Louis Nashelsky*
* **Op Amp Applications Handbook** — *Walt Jung (Analog Devices)*
* **Linear Circuit Design Handbook** — *Hank Zumbahlen (Analog Devices)*
* **Data Conversion Handbook** — *Walt Kester (Analog Devices)*
* **The Signal: A Compendium of Blog Posts on Precision Analog** — *Texas Instruments*

### 📁 [02_Signal_Integrity](./02_Signal_Integrity/)
Covers high-speed digital circuit design, signal integrity, and high-frequency board layouts:
* **High Speed Signal Propagation: Advanced Black Magic** — *Howard Johnson, Martin Graham*
* **High-Speed Layout Guidelines (SCAA082)** — *Texas Instruments*
* **High-Speed Interface Layout Guidelines (SPRAAR7)** — *Texas Instruments*
* **Signal Integrity Rules of Thumb** — *Eric Bogatin*
* **Essential Rules of Thumb for Signal Integrity** — *Eric Bogatin*
* **Bouncing Out Ground Bounce** — *Eric Bogatin*

### 📁 [03_Embedded_Systems](./03_Embedded_Systems/)
Includes documentation for microcontrollers, hardware platform specifications, and embedded architecture:
* **Embedded System Design**

### 📁 [04_Electrical_Engineering](./04_Electrical_Engineering/)
Comprehensive textbooks on general electrical engineering concepts and principles:
* **Electrical Engineering: Principles and Applications**
* **Principles and Applications of Electrical Engineering** — *Giorgio Rizzoni*

### 📁 [05_Power_Integrity](./05_Power_Integrity/)
Covers power integrity fundamentals: decoupling, grounding, regulator design, and power supply PCB layout:
* **An IC Amplifier User's Guide to Decoupling, Grounding, and Making Things Go Right for a Change (AN-202)** — *Paul Brokaw (Analog Devices)*
* **Grounding for Low-and-High-Frequency Circuits (AN-345)** — *Analog Devices*
* **Grounding Data Converters and Demystifying AGND and DGND (MT-031)** — *Walt Kester (Analog Devices)*
* **Decoupling Techniques (MT-101)** — *Analog Devices*
* **Linear Regulators: Theory of Operation and Compensation (AN-1148)** — *Texas Instruments*
* **Five Steps to a Great PCB Layout for a Step-Down Converter (SLYT614)** — *Texas Instruments*

### 📁 [06_EMC_EMI](./06_EMC_EMI/)
Covers electromagnetic compatibility, EMI/RFI fundamentals, shielding, and low-emission PCB design:
* **Simple Success With Conducted EMI From DC-DC Converters (AN-2162)** — *Texas Instruments*
* **EMI, RFI, and Shielding Concepts (MT-095)** — *Analog Devices*
* **PCB Design Guidelines for Reduced EMI (SZZA009)** — *Texas Instruments*
